## Public responsibilities

The Keepers coordinate civic work, represent their communities, offer knowledge and aid, and respond to threats affecting the shared settlement. Alaric currently sponsors the party's investigation of the northern mountain pass.

## Succession

Alaric's father, Gauri, was the prior Highborne Keeper and died within the last fifteen years. The exact date remains unknown. See [[05 Public Records/Known Keeper Succession|Known Keeper Succession]].
