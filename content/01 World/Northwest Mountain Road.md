# Northwest Mountain Road

[[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Sunday Vasudrani/Northern Expedition|Northern Expedition]]

The Northwest Mountain Road is a cart route from the Settled Town toward Ka'thar's remote home territory and the northern mountain pass. Mata of the Nine Keepers provided route guidance.

Known features include a first-fork camp, rain-soaked switchbacks, steep cliff turns, and a broad seasonal flashwash. During the current expedition, worsening rain turned the wash into sixty to seventy feet of icy water, with its fastest section near the center.

A very large living oak, now called the [[02 People/People Met on the Expedition#Riverbank Treant|Riverbank Treant]], stands at the far bank and protects it from erosion. A side trail beyond the crossing leads to the [[01 World/Frostbound Grove|Frostbound Grove]].
