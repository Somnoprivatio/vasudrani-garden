# Only Settled Town

[[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]] · [[03 Factions/Nine Keepers|Nine Keepers]]

The **Only Settled Town** is Vasudrani's centralized civic settlement. It serves as a place where the nine communities can trade, research, receive aid, and meet their representatives.

## Known districts and institutions

- **Trade and Market District** — shops, stalls, supplies, and public exchange.
- **Keepers' manor and meeting spaces** — administrative and communal work of the Nine Keepers.
- [[01 World/Fungal Clearing and Hall of Records#Hall of Records|Hall of Records]] — books, bestiaries, and magical research.
- **Karn Grudgebell's forge** — blacksmithing and purification work.

The town's official proper name, population, wall plan, and full government beyond the Keepers have not yet been established in the public record.
