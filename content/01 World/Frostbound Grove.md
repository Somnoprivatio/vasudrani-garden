# Frostbound Grove

[[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]] · [[04 Lore/Northern Snow Maiden Folklore|Northern Snow Maiden Folklore]]

![[90 Assets/Places/Frostbound Grove.png]]

The Frostbound Grove lies beyond a side trail from the northwest river crossing. The party reached it on 03 Aranyaal, Year 70.

The grove contains a waterlogged cottage, small stable, frozen rock face, western waterfall, broad ankle-deep creek, and hundreds of transparent lotus and lily flowers preserved in full spring bloom. The air was about fifteen degrees colder than the surrounding forest. Ancient magic threads through the place, and some trees move abnormally.

A joyous frost-blue elven woman danced across the water with a pure-white snow owl, then welcomed the travelers peacefully. She could not remember her own name and said she had not seen her grandmother in thousands of seasons. She opened the cottage's magical ice and provided warmth and food for the horses.

The cottage's side rooms are larger than the exterior permits. Its arrangement suggests a household of about five people. The resident's identity, the grove's full history, and the cause of its preservation are not publicly established.
