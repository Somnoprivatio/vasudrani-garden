# World Overview

[[00 Home/Start Here|Home]] · [[01 World/Places Index|Places]]

Vasudrani's current civilization is approximately seventy years old. The best-documented civic center is the [[01 World/Only Settled Town|Only Settled Town]], where representatives of nine communities gather as the [[03 Factions/Nine Keepers|Nine Keepers]].

Beyond the town, records remain local and incomplete. The current campaign has established a [[01 World/Northwest Mountain Road|northwest mountain route]], a [[01 World/Fungal Clearing and Hall of Records#Fungal Clearing|bioluminescent fungal clearing]], and a [[01 World/Frostbound Grove|Frostbound Grove]] hidden beyond a difficult river crossing.

The single moon is called [[04 Lore/Vidhu|Vidhu]]. Magic is commonly discussed through [[04 Lore/Three States of Magic|Ambient Mana, Dharma, and Azura]], though the source material has not established how uniformly every community uses this scholarly model.
