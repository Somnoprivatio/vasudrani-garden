# Fungal Clearing and Hall of Records

[[01 World/Places Index|Places Index]]

## Fungal Clearing

A clearing marked by bioluminescent mushrooms near Pin's original resting place. The first party saw and avoided a [[04 Lore/Lucidal Hawks#Giant Veil-Crested Albino Hawk|Giant Veil-Crested Albino Hawk]] in the surrounding area.

## Hall of Records

A public or party-accessible archive within the settled center. It contains bestiaries and magical research. During Session 004, the party found extensive information about the giant hawk, but a critical section concerning its metallic plumage, communication, and voluntary cooperation had been removed from the book.

The identity and motive of whoever removed the pages are not public knowledge.
