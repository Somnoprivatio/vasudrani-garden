# Known Keeper Succession

[[05 Public Records/Public Records Index|Public Records]] · [[03 Factions/Nine Keepers|Nine Keepers]]

Gauri served as the Highborne Keeper before his son, Alaric. Gauri died within the last fifteen years, and Alaric succeeded him as the Highborne representative associated with Mahagauri.

The exact date and circumstances of Gauri's death are not yet established. Complete succession lists for the other eight seats have not entered the public record.
