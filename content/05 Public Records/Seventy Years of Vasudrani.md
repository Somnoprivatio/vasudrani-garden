# Seventy Years of Vasudrani

[[05 Public Records/Public Records Index|Public Records]]

The current civilization of Vasudrani is approximately seventy years old. Publicly documented institutions center on the Settled Town, the nine represented communities, and the Nine Keepers.

The surviving record does not yet provide a complete founding chronology, a list of earlier governments, a continent map, or exact dates for most institutions. Older-world remnants and legends exist, but their complete relationship to the present has not been made public.

Current dated campaign records use **Year 70**, including the northern expedition on 02–03 Aranyaal.
