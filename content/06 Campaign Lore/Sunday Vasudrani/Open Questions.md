# Sunday Vasudrani Open Questions

[[06 Campaign Lore/Sunday Vasudrani/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

## Immediate

- Who is the Frostbound Grove resident, and why can she not remember her name?
- What created the grove's preserved spring, impossible cottage rooms, and intense cold?
- What will the party learn before sundown, and will they remain overnight?
- Can the Treant's bark become a protective charm?

## Expedition

- What lies in the northern mountain pass and Asiloman's workshops?
- How do the Oculorbs gather and transport Dharma?
- Where does the Giant Veil-Crested Albino Hawk nest, and what was removed from the research volume?
- What dangers remain between the grove, Ka'thar's home territory, and the mountains?

## Intentionally unknown

The full Snow Maiden story, any connection to current characters, the resident's true condition, the missing-page thief, and the hidden material behind the hawk's metallic feathers are not disclosed here.
