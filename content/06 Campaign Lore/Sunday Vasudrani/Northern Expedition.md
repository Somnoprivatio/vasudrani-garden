# Northern Expedition

[[06 Campaign Lore/Sunday Vasudrani/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

## Purpose

Alaric asked the party to investigate the northern mountain pass from which the Oculorbs came. He offered to cover travel costs to Ka'thar's home territory in exchange for information.

## Resources

- Two horses and a covered wagon.
- Route information from Mata.
- Nina's expertise in magical identification.
- Ka'thar's wilderness knowledge.
- The party's research on the Giant Veil-Crested Albino Hawk.

## Progress

The party survived muddy switchbacks, saved the wagon from a cliff turn, crossed the icy flashwash, and reached the Frostbound Grove with all travelers, animals, and equipment intact.

## Immediate position

The horses are warm and resting in the grove's stable. The party has not completed a long rest, received the requested soup, or finished questioning the resident. Sundown is expected in about one hour.
