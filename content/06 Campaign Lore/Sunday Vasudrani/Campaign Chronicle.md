# Sunday Vasudrani Campaign Chronicle

[[06 Campaign Lore/Sunday Vasudrani/Campaign Overview|Campaign Overview]]

## Before Session 004

The party fought Oculorbs created by Asiloman. They witnessed Alaric's advancement to Keeper, helped exhausted Keepers, and delivered recovered metallic chitin to Karn Grudgebell for purification. The Skyborne Keeper recovered and departed to help the Skyborne people return to the skies.

## Session 004 — Soup, Scholars, and Stolen Pages

**02 Aranyaal, Year 70; ended 11:08 AM.** Moshka and Nina joined the active party. Lev remained behind to help the injured. Alaric offered to fund travel if the party investigated the northern pass. In the Hall of Records, the group researched the Giant Veil-Crested Albino Hawk and discovered missing pages.

## Session 005 — Northwest Mountain Expedition

**02–03 Aranyaal; ended about 3:45 PM.** Mata provided a viable cart route and wilderness warnings. The party departed with two horses and a covered wagon, camped at the first fork, survived dangerous rainy switchbacks and a runaway wagon, and stopped at an icy flashwash to choose a crossing method.

## Session 006 — River Crossing and Frostbound Cottage

**03 Aranyaal, about 3:45–6:00 PM.** The party built a two-anchor rope guide with help from the Riverbank Treant. Curious Wisps and Water Elementals complicated the crossing, but the party did not permanently harm them. Everyone and all equipment reached the far bank. Following the Treant's directions, the party found the Frostbound Grove, met its unnamed resident and white owl, and accepted shelter for the horses.

The chronicle stops before sundown. It does not include future-session preparation or private internal memories.
