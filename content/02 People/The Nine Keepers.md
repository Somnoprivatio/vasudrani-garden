# The Nine Keepers - People

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]] · [[03 Factions/Nine Keepers|Nine Keepers as a faction]]

## Alaric, Son of Gauri

The middle-aged male Human Highborne Keeper, titled **the Ivory Regent**. Alaric succeeded his father, Gauri, and currently sponsors the northern-pass investigation.

## Siddhidhatri Vedamaya

The ageless Clank Loreborne Keeper, known as **Siddha** and titled **Keeper of the Ninth Lamp**. Siddha uses they/them pronouns and teaches Nina Astorious.

## Brahmacharini Tapasvra

The Human Orderborne Keeper, known as **Charini** and titled **Vowbound Keeper**. Charini is a male-presenting woman who uses she/they pronouns.

## Shailaputri Girivara

The female Giant Ridgeborne Keeper, known as **Shaila** and titled **Keeper of the First Stone**.

## Chandraghanta Sagarika

The female Galapa Seaborne Keeper, known as **Chandra** and titled **Tidebell Keeper**.

## Kushmanda Arkajyoti

The nonbinary, insect-like Faerie Skyborne Keeper, known as **Ushma** and titled **She Who Lit the Sky-Egg**. Ushma uses they/them pronouns.

## Kalaratri Nishacari

The female Katari Slyborne Keeper, known as **Ratri**. A formal public title has not yet been recorded.

## Skandamata Pantharakshini

The female Firbolg Wanderborne Keeper, known as **Mata**. She supplied the current expedition with route knowledge. A formal public title has not yet been recorded.

## Katyayani Vanashakti

The female Elf Wildborne Keeper, known as **Katya** and titled **Lion of the Green Wild**.
