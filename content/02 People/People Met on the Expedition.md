# People Met on the Expedition

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]] · [[06 Campaign Lore/Sunday Vasudrani/Northern Expedition|Northern Expedition]]

## Riverbank Treant

An approximately 115-foot living oak rooted at the far side of the icy flashwash. It protects the riverbank from encroachment and finds movement physically taxing. It voluntarily anchored the party's guide rope, warned that kindness toward the water spirits would be returned, and gave Moshka naturally fallen bark for a possible protective charm.

## Frostbound Grove resident

An unnamed frost-blue elven woman accompanied by a pure-white snow owl. She appeared joyful and kind, danced upon the shallow water, welcomed the party, opened the cottage's ice, and sheltered their horses. She said she had forgotten her name, had not had visitors for a very long time, and weakened outside the grove.

The resident's true identity and the cause of her condition have not been established to the full party.
