# Keepers and Civic Figures

[[02 People/People Index|People Index]] · [[03 Factions/Nine Keepers|Nine Keepers]]

## Gauri

The former Highborne Keeper and father of Alaric. Gauri died within the last fifteen years; the exact date is not publicly established. Alaric succeeded him.

## Karn Grudgebell

A giant master blacksmith serving the wealthy district of the Only Settled Town. Karn is known as an exacting smith with Highborne patrons. The Keepers directed the party to bring Oculorb chitinous armor to Karn for purification after the battle.

## Bhadra Kiln-Horn

An older Ridgeborne Firbolg called **the Honest Hammer**. Bhadra is the Trade and Market District's town blacksmith, repairs tools and wagons, and teaches festival forging. He keeps the first good pieces made by students in a box called *First Sparks*. Bhadra is not one of the Nine Keepers.

## Madhupushpa Vani

Madhupushpa “Madu” Vani is the **Garden-Tender of the Trade District**: gardener, florist, herbal tender, and arranger of festival flowers. She is an insect-and-faerie humanoid with bee-like features and colorful butterfly wings and is also known as Grandmother Bloom and Honey-Auntie Madu. Madu is not one of the Nine Keepers.

## Asiloman, the Sword-Haired

A known threat associated with workshops in the mountains north of Ka'thar's home territory. Alaric told the party that Asiloman sends minions to gather Dharma, consumes what they return with, and reforges those minions. See [[04 Lore/Oculorbs|Oculorbs]].
