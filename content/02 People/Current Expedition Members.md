# Current Expedition Members

## Pin

A current player character with strong interests in books, mysteries, and magic who participated in the Hall of Records investigation.

## Ka'thar

A Katari from a remote wilderness community. Ka'thar guides the expedition toward his home territory and applies practical tracking, hunting, and wilderness knowledge. Detailed clan history and family information remain character-limited.

## Moshka

A kindly older woman who travels in a mortar and uses a pestle as mobility and grappling equipment. She is an alchemist, prepares food readily, communicates well with natural beings, and travels with a cat named Dima. Her unexplained memories are not public.

## Nina Astorious

A Loreborne wizard and scholar played by Yuki. Nina studies with Siddha and is known for books, mysteries, magic, and research. She articulated the [[04 Lore/Three States of Magic|three states of magic]] and joined the expedition to identify magical phenomena.
