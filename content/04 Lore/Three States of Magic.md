# Three States of Magic

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

Nina Astorious described three states of magic according to whether and how magic is being used.

| State | Public definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Ambient Mana** | Magic that is not being used at all. |
| **Dharma** | Magic being used for beneficial ends. |
| **Azura** | Magic being used negatively or harmfully. |

The formal model is confirmed, but its acceptance by every community has not been established.

> [!important] Distinct terms
> **Azura** is a state of magic. **Asura** is a different word used for adversarial figures such as Asiloman. They are not alternate spellings.
