# Lucidal Hawks

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]] · [[01 World/Fungal Clearing and Hall of Records#Hall of Records|Hall of Records]]

The Lucidal hawk family includes the rare **Giant Veil-Crested Albino Hawk**, an immense non-migratory subspecies.

![[90 Assets/Creatures/Giant Veil-Crested Albino Hawk.png]]

## Giant Veil-Crested Albino Hawk

- Torso approximately 45 feet long; a mature wingspan can exceed 100 feet.
- Luminous white plumage with bright metallic tips.
- Permanent high-altitude territory and winter aerie rather than migration.
- Highly territorial but normally escalates through observation, warning displays, ground claims, and false passes before attacking.
- Prefers mountain shelves, broad ridgelines, open hunting ground, reliable updrafts, and clear approach lanes.

Safe observers remain below or across the valley from a nest, avoid eggs and prey caches, and retreat laterally or downhill when warned. Ordinary capture and domestication are considered ineffective and dangerous; any cooperation would need to be voluntary.

Pages concerning the metallic plumage, communication, and a method of earning cooperation were missing from the Hall of Records volume. Their contents and the person who removed them are unknown.
