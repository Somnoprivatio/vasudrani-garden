# Northern Snow Maiden Folklore

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]] · [[01 World/Frostbound Grove|Frostbound Grove]]

Northern folklore speaks of **Grandfather Frost**, an **Old Crone**, and a blue-white **Snow Maiden** accompanied by an owl and bound to a cottage through an old curse. In the remembered story, family love, winter, sacrifice, and separation are inseparable.

Only this compact form is currently suitable for a public legend guide. Names, the complete family tree, the exact bargain, the source of the old settlement's destruction, and any claim connecting the story to living people remain unrevealed.

The party's encounter with an unnamed frost-blue woman and white owl resembles the legend, but resemblance is not confirmation of identity.
