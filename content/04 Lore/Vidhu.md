# Vidhu

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]]

**Vidhu** is the single moon of Vasudrani. References to multiple moons in older or imported material are not part of the current setting canon.

The public record has not yet established Vidhu's phases, calendar, deities, or direct magical effects.
