# Oculorbs

[[04 Lore/Lore Index|Lore Index]] · [[02 People/Keepers and Civic Figures#Asiloman, the Sword-Haired|Asiloman]]

Oculorbs are constructs created by Asiloman in workshops located in the mountains north of Ka'thar's home territory. The party fought them before Session 004.

Alaric described their known cycle:

1. Asiloman sends Oculorbs or associated minions to gather Dharma.
2. The filled minions return to him.
3. He consumes the gathered Dharma.
4. He reforges the minions for further use.

Recovered remains included metallic chitinous armor. The Keepers ordered the material delivered to Karn Grudgebell for purification, and the party completed that delivery. The constructs' exact senses, weapons, communication, and material composition remain unknown.
