# Reader Guide

Return to [[00 Home/Start Here|Vasudrani Player Guide]].

## Information levels

- **World reference** is broadly public or available through ordinary research.
- **Public record** covers shared civic history and known succession.
- **Campaign lore** records what the current party has collectively witnessed or received.
- Statements made by a character are presented as that character's testimony unless independently established.

## Spoiler boundary

This vault is built by selection, not by hiding GM text inside public pages. It excludes secret names, concealed identities, unshared memories, adversary conversions, unrevealed motives, hidden magical materials, encounter branches, and the full explanation of the Frostbound Grove.

## Unknowns

An omitted fact is either not established or not public. Do not treat a blank as an invitation to infer the GM answer.
