# Vasudrani Player Guide

Welcome to **Vasudrani**, a young world where communities are represented by the [[03 Factions/Nine Keepers|Nine Keepers]], magic is understood through use and consequence, and much of the land beyond the settled center remains unknown.

This vault is a high-level, player-safe reference. It contains confirmed public setting information, researchable lore, and a separate chronicle of events witnessed by the active Sunday Vasudrani party. Private character histories, unrevealed identities, hidden corruption, GM preparation, and the answers behind current mysteries are omitted.
